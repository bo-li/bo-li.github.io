Research

Current research directions

My research program centers on three complementary directions that connect fundamental physical chemistry with modern AI.

Research I

Hybrid ML Potentials for Solution Thermodynamics

Combining electronic-structure theory, molecular simulation, and machine learning to predict solvation, reaction, and transport properties in chemically complex liquid environments.

Research II

Physics-Informed Universal Potentials

Developing transferable machine-learned interatomic potentials for disordered ionic systems, including molten salts, glasses, and solid-state ionic materials.

Research III

Generative AI for Inverse Molecular Design

Integrating property prediction, chemical reactivity models, and generative molecular representations to discover molecules with targeted thermodynamic and chemical properties.
